Privacy in Brief

GBTCOINESE No.1 requires no account and includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Game progress, best scores, stars, tutorial flags, and settings for sound effects and vibration & haptics are stored locally on your device. The app is designed as a self-contained casual entertainment experience without gambling, betting, or real-money features.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the developer and operator of GBTCOINESE No.1 (“GBTCOINESE No.1,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the GBTCOINESE No.1 iOS application. It applies to the current version of the app and its in-app agreement and privacy pages.

The app provides a collection of casual workshop mini games, including Tower Wave Upgrade, Slice & Fit, Beat Tap, Fill the Gap, Extra Letter, and Fuel Run. It also includes the Tidal Shell Realm hub, short tutorials, score tracking, stars, sound effects and vibration & haptics settings, and local progress summaries (Total Stars, Minis Played, Recent).

2. Information You Do Not Need to Provide

GBTCOINESE No.1 does not require account registration, login, identity verification, or payment information. The current app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, contacts, precise or approximate location, microphone, or advertising identifier.

  • No account profile is created.
  • No gambling, betting, cash prizes, wagering, or real-money rewards are offered.
  • No advertising, cross-app tracking, or behavioral profiling is used.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDK is integrated in the current app.
  • No payment credentials or financial accounts are connected.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The app uses local device storage for information needed to remember your choices and gameplay progress. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Game progress, best scores, stars, and recently played mini games.
  • Tutorial completion flags for each mini game.
  • Settings choices such as sound effects and vibration & haptics.
  • Local progress summaries shown in the Tidal Shell Realm hub and Settings.

This information is not an account held by us and is not synchronized by the app to an account or cloud database operated by us. It remains on your device unless it is included in an operating-system backup under settings controlled by you and the platform provider.

4. Optional Permissions

Some device permissions may be requested only when you choose to use a supported feature. Optional camera or photo access, if requested by your device, is used only when you choose to provide an image within a supported feature. You can deny camera access or change camera permission later in iOS Settings.

Device authentication, when used, is handled by iOS using the authentication method configured on your device, such as Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode. GBTCOINESE No.1 does not receive your face image, fingerprint data, device passcode, or biometric template. The app receives only an authentication success or failure result.

5. How Information Is Used and Disclosed

Information described in this policy is used only to provide requested features, remember on-device choices, display gameplay progress, and improve your in-app experience. We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not disclose app information to advertising networks or data brokers and do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

6. Retention and Security

Local records remain on your device until you remove or reset them through available app controls, clear relevant system data, or uninstall the app. You can use Reset Tutorials or Reset All Progress from Settings inside the app.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information involved, but no device, transmission, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

  • Turn sound effects or vibration & haptics on or off in Settings.
  • Reset tutorials with “Reset Tutorials” or clear all scores and stars with “Reset All Progress” in Settings.
  • Remove all app data by deleting GBTCOINESE No.1 from your device.
  • Manage optional camera permission in iOS Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection. Because the app has no user accounts and we may be unable to associate stored data with you, we may not hold identifiable information that can be returned or deleted. You may still contact us, and we will respond as required by applicable law.

GBTCOINESE No.1 is a casual entertainment app suitable for broad audiences and is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where required by local law. We do not knowingly create accounts for or solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can review the matter.
